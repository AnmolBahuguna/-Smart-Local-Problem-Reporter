Why CivicReport

Built for Accountability, Designed for Citizens

A transparent, end-to-end grievance redressal workflow — from the moment you report to the moment it's resolved.

📝

Guided Complaint Filing

A simple 3-step process — choose the category, describe the issue with a photo, review and submit. You get a tracking ID instantly.

💡

Instant Action Guidance

Every complaint comes with rule-based next steps: the right authority to contact, escalation paths, and emergency numbers.

📊

End-to-End Tracking

Follow your complaint through Pending → In Progress → Resolved with official notes from the handling department at every stage.

⭐

Feedback & Rating

Rate the resolution once your issue is closed. Citizen feedback drives department performance monitoring.
